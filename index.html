The Story

From the Stage to the Strategy Room

I started in Nollywood. Nearly two decades on set, in front of cameras, performing for audiences who needed to believe what they were watching.

That work taught me something most brand strategists never learn — a story only works if the person watching it feels something real.

Today, I apply that same discipline to brands, founders, and businesses. I help them find the story at the centre of what they're building, shape it into something that moves people, and deliver it in ways that close deals, attract investors, and build lasting presence.

I am the intersection of performance and strategy. That is not a metaphor. It is a methodology.
